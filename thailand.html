    Paying and getting around

    MRT

    Tap your Quicksilver or Google Pay at the gate. All four MRT lines and the Airport Rail Link take contactless Visa and Mastercard. Tap in and out with the same card or phone.

    BTS Skytrain

    Needs a Rabbit card: ฿200 at any BTS ticket office (฿100 card fee + ฿100 to ride), bring your passport. Top up ฿100–4,000 in cash. Where BTS meets MRT you exit and pay again.

    Shops and street food

    Google Pay with your US cards works wherever contactless is taken. Street stalls want cash or PromptPay QR, which needs a Thai bank account. Try TrueMoney once you have a Thai number.

    DTV rules

    • Approved. Good for 5 years from the approval date, up to 180 days per entry.
    • Extension: at the end of each 180 days, extend once inside Thailand for 180 more (about ฿1,900), then leave and come back.
    • Bring: the e-visa PDF (printed and on your phone), Watchara enrollment proof, proof of funds. Travel on the passport the visa was issued to.

    Tax

    • 180+ days in Thailand in a calendar year makes you a Thai tax resident, whatever your visa. Leaving and coming back doesn't reset the count.
    • Foreign income earned since 2024 is taxable when brought into Thailand. VA disability is generally taxable only by the US under the treaty.
    • Plan: send only VA money to Thailand from an account that holds nothing else; leave savings and interest in the US. Confirm with a US–Thai expat tax specialist.
